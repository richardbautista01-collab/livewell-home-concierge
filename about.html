ABOUT LIVEWELL HOME CONCIERGE

Helping Families.
Supporting Independence.

LiveWell Home Concierge helps older adults remain comfortable and independent in the homes they know while giving their families a trusted local presence they can rely on.

OUR STORY

A Different Kind of Home Support.

LiveWell Home Concierge was founded with one simple mission: to help older adults remain comfortable, confident, and independent in the homes they love while providing families with greater peace of mind.

We understand that managing a home can become more challenging over time. Everyday responsibilities, home maintenance, appointments, organization, and coordinating support can create stress for both older adults and their families.

And for families who live across town, across the GTA, or simply have busy lives of their own, being there for every little thing isn't always possible.

That's where LiveWell comes in.

We provide trusted home concierge services designed to simplify the practical details, help families stay connected, and make staying at home easier to manage.

OUR FOUNDING TEAM

Experience From Different Perspectives.

LiveWell was founded by professionals with complementary backgrounds in corporate finance, business advisory, nursing, and senior support.

Our Founder brings experience in corporate finance, business advisory, and strategic planning, helping individuals and organizations navigate complex decisions and long-term planning.

Our broader founding team also includes Registered Nurses (RNs), Registered Practical Nurses (RPNs), and Personal Support Workers (PSWs) with experience working with older adults and within retirement home environments.

Together, these perspectives help us understand both sides of the equation: the practical realities of managing a home and the importance of supporting older adults and their families.

What That Means for Your Family

  • A practical understanding of home and household needs
  • Experience coordinating people, services, and details
  • An understanding of the challenges families face

LiveWell Home Concierge does not provide medical care, nursing, personal support, or other healthcare services. Our healthcare-related experience informs how we understand the needs of families, but LiveWell's role is focused on non-medical home concierge and coordination support.

OUR COMMITMENT

We believe home is more than a building. It's familiarity, independence, memories, routines, and the comfort of being somewhere you know.

When you can't be there for everything, LiveWell can be.

What LiveWell Is — And Isn't

LiveWell Home Concierge provides non-medical home concierge, household coordination, maintenance coordination, and practical support services.

We do not provide medical care, nursing, personal support, emergency response, professional home inspections, engineering services, or other regulated professional services.

When a situation requires specialized or licensed expertise, LiveWell can help coordinate the appropriate third-party professional.

Our role is simple: help with the practical details, keep things organized, and help families stay connected.
