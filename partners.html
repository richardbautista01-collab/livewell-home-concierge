Partner With LiveWell

You support the person. We support the home. Together, we can help seniors live safely and independently at home.

We Don't Replace Your Role — We Support It

LiveWell respects the important work performed by PSWs and healthcare professionals. Our services are designed to complement your support by helping families with the home-related challenges that often fall outside your role.

Why We Partner With PSWs

Families often need more than personal support. They may need help managing the home, coordinating repairs, organizing household needs, or keeping family members informed.

LiveWell provides a trusted home concierge solution while allowing PSWs to continue focusing on the important support they provide.

How We Support Families Together

  • Home check-ins and household support
  • Maintenance coordination
  • Contractor scheduling and follow-up
  • Home organization assistance
  • Seasonal home preparation
  • Communication and updates for families

Who We Partner With

  • Personal Support Workers (PSWs)
  • Registered Practical Nurses (RPNs)
  • Registered Nurses (RNs)
  • Senior-focused professionals
  • Community organizations
  • Retirement and aging-at-home networks

A Partnership Built On Trust

We understand that trust is earned. PSWs develop meaningful relationships with the seniors and families they support, and we respect the role you play.

Our goal is simple: create a stronger support network around seniors by working together.
