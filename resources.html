Aging at Home: What Families Should Know

Many seniors want to remain in the homes they love for as long as possible. With the right planning and support, aging at home can provide comfort, independence, and peace of mind.

LiveWell helps families create proactive plans by coordinating household needs, identifying potential concerns, and providing reliable ongoing support.

10 Signs Your Loved One May Need More Support

  • The home is becoming harder to maintain
  • Small repairs are being ignored
  • Yard work or seasonal tasks are becoming difficult
  • Safety concerns are appearing around the home
  • Family members are spending more time coordinating support
  • Household organization is becoming overwhelming
  • Technology or appointments are becoming challenging
  • There has been a fall or safety incident
  • The home no longer feels as manageable
  • Family members feel concerned because they cannot always be nearby

Senior Home Safety Checklist

Inside The Home

  • Clear walkways and remove trip hazards
  • Improve lighting in hallways and stairs
  • Check smoke and carbon monoxide detectors
  • Install safety equipment when needed
  • Keep emergency contacts accessible

Outside The Home

  • Maintain entrances and walkways
  • Address seasonal hazards
  • Check outdoor lighting
  • Maintain steps and railings

Preparing Your Home For Retirement

A home should continue supporting your lifestyle as your needs change.

  • Reduce maintenance responsibilities
  • Improve accessibility
  • Create emergency plans
  • Organize important documents
  • Build a trusted support network
