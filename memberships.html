Every Member Gets an Annual Home Walkthrough

Once a year, LiveWell walks through the home with you to identify visible maintenance items and areas that may need attention.

We'll help identify things that may need to be repaired, maintained, replaced, or monitored and can coordinate the appropriate service provider when requested.

The LiveWell Home Walkthrough is a visual, non-invasive maintenance review. It is not a professional home inspection, engineering assessment, safety certification, or diagnosis of concealed defects.

What Counts as Basic Handyman Help?

Our included handyman hours are intended for small, practical household tasks.

01

Small Household Tasks

Changing light bulbs, replacing batteries, tightening cabinet hardware, hanging pictures, and similar basic tasks.

02

Minor Adjustments

Small non-skilled household adjustments and basic assembly that do not require specialized equipment or expertise.

03

Need a Professional?

If a job requires a licensed trade, specialized expertise, or major repair, LiveWell can help coordinate the appropriate professional.

04

Materials Are Separate

Your included handyman time covers eligible labour. Bulbs, hardware, supplies, materials, and third-party services are paid for separately.
