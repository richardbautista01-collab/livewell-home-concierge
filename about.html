About LiveWell Home Concierge

Supporting seniors, connecting families, and helping homes remain safe, comfortable, and well managed.

Our Story

LiveWell Home Concierge was founded with one mission: to help seniors remain comfortable, confident, and independent in the homes they love while providing families with peace of mind.

We understand that managing a home can become more challenging over time. Everyday responsibilities, home maintenance, organization, and coordinating support can create stress for seniors and their families.

LiveWell provides trusted home concierge services designed to simplify these challenges and help families stay connected.

Our Founding Team

LiveWell was founded by professionals with complementary backgrounds in finance, business, nursing, and senior support.

Our Founder brings experience in corporate finance, business advisory, and strategic planning, helping individuals and organizations navigate complex decisions and long-term planning.

Our founding team also includes Registered Nurses (RNs), Registered Practical Nurses (RPNs), and Personal Support Workers (PSWs) with years of experience working with seniors and within retirement home environments.

While LiveWell does not provide medical care or healthcare services, our team's experience provides valuable insight into the challenges seniors and families face when planning for safe and comfortable aging at home.

What We Do

  • Home check-ins and wellness visits
  • Household organization and support
  • Maintenance coordination
  • Contractor coordination
  • Seasonal home preparation
  • Family communication and updates

Our Commitment

We believe seniors should have the opportunity to remain in the homes they love while having the right support around them.

Our role is to provide a reliable presence, help families stay proactive, and make managing a home easier.
