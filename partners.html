LiveWell respects the important work performed by PSWs, healthcare professionals, and senior-focused organizations. Our services are designed to complement your support by helping families with the home-related challenges that often fall outside your role.

Building a Stronger Support Network

Families often need support beyond personal care. By working together, we can help address both the needs of the person and the needs of the home.

Why We Partner With PSWs

Families often need more than personal support. They may need help managing the home, coordinating repairs, organizing household needs, or keeping family members informed.

LiveWell provides a trusted home concierge solution while allowing PSWs to continue focusing on the important support they provide.

🏡

How We Support Families Together

  • Home check-ins and household support
  • Maintenance coordination
  • Contractor scheduling and follow-up
  • Home organization assistance
  • Seasonal home preparation
  • Communication and updates for families

A Partnership Built On Trust

WORKING TOGETHER

Better Support Starts With Collaboration.

Aging at home often involves a network of people helping in different ways. A PSW may support personal care. Healthcare professionals may support health needs. Families provide love and oversight.

LiveWell focuses on the home — helping coordinate the maintenance, household tasks, and practical details that can otherwise become another responsibility for families and care providers.

You Support the Person. We Support the Home.

Together, we can help families create a more complete aging-at-home support system.

Our role is to complement your work — not replace it.
