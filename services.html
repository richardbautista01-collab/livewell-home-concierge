Every Member Gets an Annual Home Walkthrough

Once a year, we walk through the home with you and look for visible maintenance items that may need attention.

We'll identify things that may need to be repaired, maintained, replaced, or monitored and can help coordinate the appropriate service when requested.

The LiveWell Home Walkthrough is a visual, non-invasive maintenance review. It is not a professional home inspection, engineering assessment, safety certification, or diagnosis of concealed defects.

A Concierge, Not a Contractor.

LiveWell coordinates and supports the practical needs of your home. We do not provide medical care, nursing, personal support, emergency response, licensed trade services, engineering services, professional home inspections, or other regulated professional services.

When specialized work is required, we can help coordinate an appropriate third-party provider. The provider remains responsible for their own work, licensing, insurance and professional obligations.
