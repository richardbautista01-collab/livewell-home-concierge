WHY LIVEWELL EXISTS

OUR STORY

A Different Kind of Home Support.

Built around a simple idea: families shouldn't have to manage every detail alone.

LiveWell Home Concierge was founded with one simple mission: to help older adults remain comfortable, confident, and independent in the homes they love while providing families with greater peace of mind.

We understand that managing a home can become more challenging over time. Everyday responsibilities, repairs, maintenance, appointments, contractors, organization, and unexpected issues can create stress for both older adults and their families.

For families who live across town, across the GTA, or simply have busy lives of their own, being there for every little thing isn't always possible.

That's where LiveWell comes in.

We provide a trusted local presence that helps coordinate the practical details of the home, keeps families informed, and makes staying at home easier to manage.

We don't replace the family. We make being there easier.

OUR FOUNDING TEAM

Experience From Different Perspectives.

LiveWell was founded by professionals with complementary backgrounds in corporate finance, business advisory, nursing, and senior support.

Our Founder brings experience in corporate finance, business advisory, and strategic planning, helping individuals and organizations navigate complex decisions and long-term planning.

Our broader founding team also includes Registered Nurses (RNs), Registered Practical Nurses (RPNs), and Personal Support Workers (PSWs) with experience working with older adults and within retirement home environments.

Together, these perspectives help us understand both sides of the equation: the practical realities of managing a home and the importance of supporting older adults and their families.

Clear About Our Role.

Families should always know exactly what LiveWell does and where our role begins and ends.

LIVEWELL IS

Your Local Home Concierge.

LiveWell provides non-medical home concierge, household coordination, maintenance coordination, and practical support services.

When something needs to get done around the home, we help coordinate the people, appointments, access, follow-ups, and communication required to keep things moving.

LIVEWELL IS NOT

A Healthcare Provider.

We do not provide medical care, nursing, personal support, emergency response, professional home inspections, engineering services, or other regulated professional services.

When a situation requires specialized or licensed expertise, LiveWell can help coordinate the appropriate third-party professional.
