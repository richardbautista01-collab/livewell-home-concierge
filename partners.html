THE VALUE OF AGING AT HOME

Helping Clients Stay Home Can Mean More Than Just Comfort.

For many older adults, remaining in their own home is an important part of maintaining independence, routine and quality of life.

But staying home successfully requires more than healthcare or personal support. The home itself needs to be maintained, monitored and cared for.

That's the gap LiveWell helps fill.

The longer your client can safely remain at home, the longer your organization can continue supporting them in the environment they know.

LiveWell helps address practical household needs before they become bigger problems — creating another layer of support around the client.

We help make aging at home more sustainable.

WORKING TOGETHER

Better Outcomes Start With Collaboration.

Aging at home often involves a network of people helping in different ways. A PSW may provide personal support. A nurse may monitor health needs. A therapist may help maintain mobility and independence. Families provide love and oversight.

LiveWell focuses on the home — coordinating the maintenance, repairs, household tasks and practical details that can otherwise become another responsibility for families and care providers.

You Bring Your Expertise. We Bring Home Support.

Together, we can create a more complete support system around the older adults and families you serve.

Our goal is simple: help people remain safely and comfortably at home for as long as possible.
