INCLUDED WITH EVERY MEMBERSHIP

An Annual Home Walkthrough

Every LiveWell membership includes an annual walkthrough of the home to identify visible maintenance items that may need attention.

We can identify things that may need to be repaired, maintained, replaced or monitored and, when requested, help coordinate the appropriate service provider.

The LiveWell Home Walkthrough is a visual, non-invasive maintenance review. It is not a professional home inspection, engineering assessment, safety certification, or diagnosis of concealed defects.

A Concierge, Not a Contractor.

LiveWell is a home concierge and coordination service. We do not provide medical care, nursing, personal support, emergency response, licensed trade services, engineering services, professional home inspections or other regulated professional services.

When specialized work is required, we can help coordinate an appropriate third-party provider. The provider remains responsible for their own work, licensing, insurance and professional obligations.
